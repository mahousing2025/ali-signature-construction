Our Process

Simple, organized, and accountable — from the first call to the final walkthrough. No surprises, no runaround.

01

Free Estimate

We gather details, photos, and measurements to fully understand your goals, scope, and timeline before providing a quote.

02

Scope & Budget

Clear, written scope of work with material options and transparent pricing — so you know exactly what you're getting.

03

Scheduling

We lock the schedule, coordinate materials delivery, and line up required licensed trade partners ahead of time.

04

Permits (if needed)

We guide you through the permit process, submit applications, and coordinate required inspections at every stage.

05

Construction

Owner on-site, clean jobsite, organized workflow, and quality checks throughout every phase of the build.

06

Final Walkthrough

We walk the job together, complete any punch list items, and don't close out until you're fully satisfied.
